Start with the source of the water

Water damage in Uniondale, NY follows a handful of patterns, and each one changes how the crew extracts, dries and documents the loss. Pick the group below that matches your situation and open the page for the details. Each page explains the signs, the drying plan and the limits of what a restoration crew does.

If nothing fits, call and describe what you see. The dispatcher will point you to the right work and tell you what to do while you wait. The line is staffed 24/7.

Pipes and plumbing leaks

A burst pipe floods fast, while a frozen pipe often fails after a thaw, when the ice melts and the split opens. Slower failures do just as much harm, such as a leaking water heater in a utility closet or a slab leak under a concrete floor. In each case the crew handles the water and the drying, and a licensed plumber repairs the line.

Appliances, kitchens and air conditioning

Worn hoses, ice-maker lines and dishwashers cause an appliance leak that can run behind cabinets for days. A clogged condensate line causes an AC leak that stains the ceiling below the air handler. Wet cabinets, toe kicks and subfloor get their own plan in kitchen water damage restoration.

Weather and the building envelope

Water entering from above is covered under ceiling water damage repair, and storm water damage cleanup covers wind-driven rain and openings in the roof or windows. Water arriving at ground level calls for flood damage restoration, basement water damage cleanup or work in a wet crawl space. We dry and clean, and a roofer or mason closes the opening.

Water removal and drying

Water extraction removes standing water with truck-mounted and portable units. Structural drying and dehumidification uses air movers and LGR or desiccant dehumidifiers to bring framing and subfloors back to dry readings. Contaminated water from a toilet or main line is handled in sewage backup cleanup, with stricter protective steps.

Floors and walls

Hardwood floors can cup or buckle, so fast drying may save them. Carpet and pad are assessed by water category before anyone decides to dry or replace them. Wet drywall wicks water upward, and wall and drywall repair covers cutting, drying and preparing the wall for rebuild.

Commercial properties

Shops, offices and multi-unit buildings, including those along Hempstead Turnpike, share ceilings and plumbing stacks, so one leak can reach several tenants. Commercial water damage restoration covers after-hours work, tenant coordination and documentation for the building's insurer.

Mold as a secondary service

Mold sometimes follows a slow or untreated leak. Mold remediation explains the sequence New York requires, where a licensed assessor writes the plan first and a separate licensed remediator carries it out. We dry and document the water side, and we do not perform mold testing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which page should I read if I do not know where the water is coming from?

Start with water extraction if water is standing, or call the 24/7 line and describe what you see. The crew finds the source with moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera, then the right work follows.

Do you repair the leaking pipe, roof or appliance?

No. We handle the water, the drying and the documentation. A licensed plumber repairs pipes, a roofer repairs roofs, and an appliance technician repairs appliances. We can tell you what the crew found so the right trade can get started.

Do you serve commercial buildings in Uniondale?

Yes. Commercial work includes after-hours access, tenant coordination and written documentation for the property's insurer. Call the 24/7 line and tell the dispatcher how many units or floors are wet.

Is mold remediation part of every water damage job?

No. Most losses are dried before mold takes hold. When mold is already present, New York law requires a licensed assessor to write a plan first, and a separate licensed remediator carries it out.
