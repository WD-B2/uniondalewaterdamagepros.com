Privacy Policy

Plain-language terms for a call-only service.

☎ (516) 845-9582

Who we are

Uniondale Water Damage Pros operates this website and a 24/7 phone dispatch line for water damage restoration in Uniondale, NY and nearby Nassau County. This site has no forms. Every request starts with a phone call.

What we collect

When you call, we may collect your phone number and the details you share about the loss, such as the address, the source of the water and the rooms affected. We use that information to dispatch a restoration professional and to follow up on your request.

Our web host may keep standard server logs, such as the pages requested and the time of the request, for security and troubleshooting.

Call recording notice

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. By continuing a call after hearing that notice, you agree to the recording.

Sharing with restoration professionals

To arrange service, we pass the details of your loss to the licensed and insured restoration professional assigned to your job. They use it only to respond to your request.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you or to remove your call details by calling the number on this site or writing to contact@uniondalewaterdamagepros.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always the one on this page.

Questions?

Call the 24/7 dispatch line and ask.
